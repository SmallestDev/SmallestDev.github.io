Try the Five Pieces rule

Two players, one screen. Get five in a row, or land on the other player's stone and roll for the square.

Battle!

  • 1Click an empty point to place your stone.
  • 2Click the other player's stone to attack it. Both sides roll a die.
  • 3Attacker rolls higher: the square becomes theirs. Otherwise the attack fails and the turn passes.
  • 4First to five in a row wins.
